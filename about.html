About Us
Discover the heart and soul of our flower shop, where passion for blooms meets a commitment to quality and customer care. Our story is one of love for flowers, dedication to craftsmanship, and a desire to bring beauty into every moment.

Our Story

Bloom & Co. was founded in 2012 by a small team of passionate florists who believed that every flower tells a story. What started as a tiny shop on a quiet street has grown into one of the most beloved floral studios in the city.

Our Mission

We create more than bouquets — we create moments. Whether it's a wedding, a birthday, or simply a Tuesday that needs brightening, we pour the same care and artistry into every arrangement.

Our Team

Our florists bring years of experience and a genuine love for their craft. Every stem is hand-selected, every bouquet thoughtfully composed. We work closely with local growers to ensure the freshest, most seasonal flowers available.
